03 / LOWER OBSERVED CUTS, NOT MIDPOINTS

A path is not an explanation of cause.

At each node, binary weighted Gini costs are ordered by exact integer cross products. Equal costs choose the lowest feature, then the lowest observed cut. Inputs at the cut go left; any larger input goes right. This lower-observed convention differs from midpoint thresholds between training values.

04 / WHAT WAS LEFT OUT MATTERS
OOB is not the held-out test.
Each tree bootstraps 808 unique training groups with replacement. Stored in-bag counts include repeated draws. A group's OOB prediction uses only trees with zero draws of that group, never the entire forest. No eligible trees means no OOB prediction. Validation and test groups do not have an OOB score. Editing features removes original-group membership until you restore a sample.
Exact four-feature copies were collapsed to one group for fitting and scoring. Original row IDs and copy counts stay in the report; distinct near-duplicates or shared physical specimens may remain. The source supplies no specimen identifiers. OOB and the final test are descriptive diagnostics under this partition, not independent real-world verification.
